# Glossary - the words the trades actually use

Gender: kk = karlkyn (m), kvk = kvenkyn (f), hk = hvorugkyn (n). Plural
given where it is irregular or often needed. Labels: [C] seen in the
mailbox corpus (clients, suppliers or Tenis), [V] verified via a
reference-source excerpt, [K] linguistic knowledge not re-verified this
session. Prefer [C] words: they are what Icelandic clients say.
