# Templates - ready Icelandic for recurring situations

Placeholders in `{braces}` must be replaced with real facts or the
sentence removed. Prices, dates, lead times and care instructions come
from the quote calculation, the project record or Tenis - never from
this file; every figure below is invented. Names are generic (Jón = male
client, Anna = female client). Gendered words are marked m/f: pick one.
All templates are written as Tenis ("ég"); the employee writes "við" and
signs as the workshop (style guide §1, §4).

### 14. Product description (web shop, label, Instagram)

Pattern: form and material · how it was made and where · glaze and
colour, one sensory observation · size or capacity · care. Four to six
short sentences. Facts only from the product record.

    Yunomi-bolli úr ljósum steinleir.
    Handrenndur í Álafossi og brenndur við háan hita. Glerungurinn er
    mattur og grábrúnn með fíngerðum tilbrigðum sem koma fram í brennslunni
    – enginn bolli er nákvæmlega eins og annar.
    Rúmar um {250} ml. {Þolir uppþvottavél.}

    Skál úr dökkum steinleir, {ø 18 cm}.
    Handmótuð og glerjuð að innan; að utan er leirinn óglerjaður og

### 17. Instagram caption pattern

One to three lines. What it is, what it is made of, one true detail,
then availability. No hashtag walls, no "!!!", no "OMG".

    Ný sería úr ofninum: yunomi-bollar úr steinleir, mattur ljósgrár
    glerungur. Fáein stykki í boði – sendið skilaboð.

## Website and CRM templates - review of 2026-09-19

Source: the quote mail and the acceptance mail the site generates
(post@preissworkshop.is), read from the mailbox on 2026-09-19. Client
name, quote number and amounts are omitted here on purpose. Overall:
good, natural Icelandic - clearly written by someone who knows the
language. Findings, ranked by what a client notices:

1. **Greeting `Sæl/l {Fullt nafn},`** -> `Sæll {Jón},` / `Sæl {Anna},`;
   unknown gender -> `Góðan dag,`. The slash form is a mail-merge tell
   and the surname does not belong in a greeting. Needs a salutation
   field on the client record, or a rule: name ends in -son -> Sæll,
   -dóttir -> Sæl, otherwise Góðan dag.
2. **Acceptance mail date `þann 2026-09-18 12:49`** -> `18. september
   2026 kl. 12:49`. ISO dates read as system output in prose.
3. **`Samtals með vsk:`** -> `Samtals með vsk.:` (abbreviation takes a
   period). Same in the acceptance mail: `með vsk.`.
4. **Phone `+354 771 1255`** is fine for a foreign reader; for Icelandic
   clients `s. 771 1255` reads more naturally. Either is acceptable.
5. **Discount label `Afsláttur`** on the quote page -> `Afsláttur (25% af
   vinnu)` when the discount is a share of labour, so the client sees what
   it is (open item from the 09-18 tracker).
6. **Closing `Kveðja,`** is fine for a system mail; `Bestu kveðjur,` is
   warmer for a first offer. Taste, not error.
7. Keep as is: "Hér er tilboð okkar ...", "Gildir til:", "Afgreiðslutími:
   Um 3 vikur frá samþykki", "Opnaðu, prentaðu eða samþykktu það hér:",
   "Spurningar? Svaraðu þessum pósti eða hringdu í ...", "Næst: við
   tímasetjum verkið og þú færð tölvupóst við hvert skref — mælingu, efni,
   framleiðslu, uppsetningu, frágang. Þú þarft ekkert að gera núna.",
   "Tilboðið er áfram aðgengilegt á tenglinum hér að neðan; verksíðan
   sýnir stöðuna." - all natural, all correct. The line items use the
   right decimal comma and unit spacing ("96,6 × 40 × 4 cm").
