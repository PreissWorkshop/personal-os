Short messages in Tenis's own register (from the Icelandic skill's corpus
notes on his English: lead with the thing, prices split and plain, one
question, one line of warmth, no exclamation marks, no superlatives). All
placeholders are in [brackets]; every price comes from `rate` or `job`,
never from the template. English; the Icelandic skill renders any of these
for an Icelandic reader. Brand rule: Preiss Workshop is precise and
premium, HelmCNC is a serious tool for people who run machines - nothing
here sells hard.

## 7. Bank call script (before any payment is missed)

1. "I want the current rate on the card and the overdraft after the
   August change, in writing, please."
2. "What would a non-indexed term loan of [amount] over [months] cost -
   rate, fees, total interest - if it replaced the card and the
   overdraft?" (Run `debt` with the quote before deciding; the five
   conditions are in `fi-and-debt.md` §7.)
3. "If a month is tight, what is the process for a reduced payment
   before the due date, and what does it cost?"
4. "Can the overdraft limit step down automatically as the balance
   falls?"
Write down the name of the person and the date. Nothing is agreed on the
phone.
