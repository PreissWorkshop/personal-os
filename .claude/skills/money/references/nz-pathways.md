# New Zealand: the visa pathways for a tradesman with a large family, from Immigration New Zealand

Built 2026-09-20 from main-pc, from Immigration New Zealand's Operational
Manual (issue of 28 August 2026), its public pages, its fees guide (May 2026)
and lbp.govt.nz. Every quotation was seen in the page text; the `nzp-*` claims
in `claims.json` re-check the manual pages. Section numbers (SR3.10 and so on)
are the manual's. Facts only, no advice; rules here change often, and an
application is decided on the instructions in force on its date. The
character rules are in `iceland-company-debt.md` section 10; trade
certificates are in `trade-qualification.md`.

## 7. What the facts allow [I]

- The route that fits an experienced tradesman without a certificate is a job
  with an accredited employer on a work visa, then residence through the
  Skilled Work Experience pathway after two years of New Zealand work at
  NZD 38.50 an hour or more. The three earlier years must be employment, not
  self-employment, inside the last ten years: how years as the director of his
  own company are treated is the first question for a licensed immigration
  adviser.
- A carpentry licence adds four points and shortens the New Zealand work
  needed on the points pathway to 18 months, at 1x pay.
- English tests are required of him (6.5 for residence, 4 for the work visa)
  despite his fluency; his partner and the 17-year-old need 5.0 or paid tuition.
- The 17-year-old stays a dependent for residence up to 20 without any test
  of support and up to 24 with one, fixed on the day the residence application
  is made; on temporary visas only up to 19.
- The investor and business categories start at one million New Zealand
  dollars. They are not a realistic route from the present position.
- Costs that are certain: NZD 6,450 for the whole family's residence
  application; NZD 1,540 for his work visa, then a separate application and
  fee for the partner and for each child.
