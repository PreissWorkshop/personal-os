## 5. Tools

No dedicated paid estimating app for architectural film was found after two
searches [G]; one shop's free web estimator has a cabinet branch
(gotwrap.com), and the free calculators found are for vehicles.

## 6. What the numbers allow [I]

- An online course priced like the ones that exist needs members in the
  hundreds to pay one salary: at 150 dollars a year, 400 members is 5,000
  dollars a month before fees, refunds and tax. No provider publishes a member
  count, so whether anyone has reached that is unknown [G].
- Installers pay ten times more for three days in a room with a certificate
  than for a year of video. The certificate belongs to the manufacturers.
- Questions to answer with a cheap test before filming: who is the buyer
  (tradesperson adding a service, or a person starting a wrapping business),
  will they pre-pay, and does a free tutorial channel gather them?
